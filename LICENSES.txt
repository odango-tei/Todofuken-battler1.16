Ver1.13 additions: rail centerlines and historic map display

Rail geometry and station data: Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, National Land Numerical Information, Railway Data N02, 2025 edition, reference date 2025-12-31. CC BY 4.0. Processed, simplified and transformed into game coordinates.
https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/gml/datalist/KsjTmplt-N02-2025.html

Historic display boundaries: Historical Administrative Area Dataset beta, ROIS-DS Center for Open Data in the Humanities. CC BY 4.0. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
